The explorer draws relationships along right-angle routes that keep clear of boxes, with each arrow leaving and entering a box at its own point, so busy boxes no longer bunch their arrows together. Journey steps follow the same routes, and steps that share one spread their numbers along it. The sidebar's sections open and close like an accordion, with a count on each; the explorer remembers which are open, and picking something on the canvas opens the section that lists it. Search stays at the top.
