A journey folds the parts of a big database it doesn't use. In the schema (ERD), the tables a journey doesn't write or read fold into one box listing them; in Rows, a table shows only the rows the journey creates, changes, or reads, plus the rows they point at, and says how many it hides. Click a folded table to bring it back, or tick "All tables" in the journey bar. Tables of rows are laid out within their datastore and reordered to keep the lines between rows from crossing: the ERP example's order-to-cash goes from over a hundred crossings to fewer than twenty.
