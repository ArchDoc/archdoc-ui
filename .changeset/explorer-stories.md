The explorer starts from what people do. Tabs: Overview (what the system is, its journeys as cards with their path, steps, repos, and decisions, who owns what, and a readable top-level map where hovering a journey lights its path), Journeys (a journey as lanes, one per participant, with numbered steps, the data each sends, and a block per outcome after a decision; the panel shows one step at a time, and "Try a sample" runs the journey and fades the branches it doesn't take), Data (every entry, its tables and lineage), and Changes (led by the PR comment's verdict, then what blocks and what to check with their fixes, who to ask, and journeys in other repos too). The map opens one level at a time and shows a box's kind by its shape; uses stay faint until a box is hovered or selected. The address keeps the tab and what's open. Colors and type sizes come from one set of tokens, light and dark.
