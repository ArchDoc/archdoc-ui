`archdoc view [--watch] [--port] [--open]` serves the new explorer: a hierarchy you can open and close, an actor focus that shows what an actor uses and owns, journeys you can step through, search, and a resizable details panel. With `--watch`, it reloads as model files change. `@archdoc/core` adds `readModelSources` and a browser-safe entry point, `@archdoc/core/browser`.
