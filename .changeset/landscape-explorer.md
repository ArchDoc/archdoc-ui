The explorer's landscape view, for `archdoc view --landscape` and the site `archdoc landscape build` writes. Group the landscape by repo, team, or domain: by team or domain, the repos are taken apart and their elements boxed by owner or domain. The sidebar lists repos with their versions, and domains with their owners, members, and journeys. Journeys that go through more than one repo are marked; stepping through one, a step that crosses into another repo (or team, or domain) is drawn bolder, the box it enters lights up, and the journey bar says where it crosses.
